# 🪄📜 The Living Spellbook Interface (LSI)

A magical, emotion-responsive habit and behavior tracking system that transforms productivity into an enchanting and immersive experience.

---

## ✨ Project Overview

The **Living Spellbook Interface (LSI)** is a fantasy-inspired digital experience where habits, emotions, and progress are represented as magical spells inside a sentient spellbook.

Instead of traditional productivity interfaces, LSI creates an emotionally adaptive environment where:
- Habits become spells
- Progress becomes magical energy
- Mood affects visuals and interactions
- The spellbook reacts like a living companion

The system focuses on emotional well-being, creativity, and gentle productivity rather than pressure-based tracking.
